      About this sample

      How it works: an observer who sees a shield of about your amount followed by an unshield of about your amount, allowing for the fee, can guess they belong together; recent matches count fully and matches older than 14 days count half. The crowd is the number of such shields. GREEN (a weighted crowd of 10 or more) means a big crowd for amount and timing only, not "private". NO MATCH means no shield of about that amount in the window, so amount matching cannot tie this unshield to a shield in it. An amount with 5 or more decimals is easy to recognise later, so round amounts blend in better.

      Covers amount + timing. Not covered: address reuse (use the command line tool with --to), wallet/fee fingerprints, your IP when your wallet broadcasts (use Tor in your wallet), z->z graph, cross-pool (Sapling<->Orchard) moves, off-chain records. The sample is a frozen copy: the command line tool syncs fresh data and cuts its own network access before it looks at your amount.
